Luxury Bungalow

A stately classical revival façade defined by strong symmetry, fluted columns, and tall arched openings. The composition balances solid wall mass with elegant glazing, while wrought-iron balconies and warm accent lighting add depth and richness. A restrained palette and formal landscaping reinforce a timeless, palace-like elegance.
